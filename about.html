Why Vozimi exists

Vozimi started with a real builder. Like most people running a site, he tracked every payment, vendor and booking in a tangle of spreadsheets, and the numbers never quite added up in time. The CRMs on the market were built for sales teams, not for someone standing on a slab at the end of the day.

So we built the opposite of a complicated CRM: one you can talk to. You say what you paid or received, and Vozimi turns it into a clean, categorised entry, then shows your cashflow and profit. That is the whole idea.

What we believe

  • Simple wins. If a feature needs a manual, it does not belong on a job site.
  • Your data is yours. Everything lives in your own Google Sheet, exportable any time.
  • Security first. Secrets never reach your phone or browser; sign-in uses short-lived tokens.
  • Honest pricing. Start free. Pay only when it is clearly saving you time and money.

How it works, in one line

Your spoken sentence goes to OpenAI GPT through a secure Google Apps Script backend, which writes a structured entry into your Google Sheet and powers your dashboard, invoices, reminders and reports.

Where we are

Vozimi is an early product built for and with real builders in India, and designed to travel. If you run a site and want it to work for your workflow, tell us.
